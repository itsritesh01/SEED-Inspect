Projects and sites

Signed-in people can open Projects. Administrators and inspectors can add fictional projects and the sites that belong to them. Viewers can look, but not change them.

Inspections

An inspection is one visit to a site. A problem found during that visit can be recorded as a finding, with one or more corrective actions.

Reports

Open an inspection and choose Download PDF. The file is created when you click it. It is not stored as a new database row.
