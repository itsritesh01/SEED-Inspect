About this prototype

SEED Inspect is a student prototype for organizing fictional engineering projects, sites, inspections, checklists, findings, corrective actions, files, and reports.

It is not a company system, and it does not approve real site work.

Who can do what

  • An administrator can manage accounts and open the audit log.
  • An administrator or inspector can add and edit projects, sites, inspections, checklists, findings, actions, and files.
  • A viewer can look, search, open the print report, and download the PDF. A viewer cannot change records.
  • There is no public registration page.

What the demonstration data is

Names such as Meera Nair, Riya Sen, Arun Das, Harbor Point Bridge, and Depot Yard are fictional. A checklist line marked Fail does not create a finding by itself. The PDF lists file names. It does not place the picture inside the PDF.

What the audit log does

It records sign-ins and changes made after Stage 11. It does not copy older practice work. It never stores a password. Opening this About page does not add an audit row.
